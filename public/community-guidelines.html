Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Keep the community safe

Do not threaten, harass, target, or encourage violence against people. Do not share private personal information or content intended to expose someone to harm.

Respect people and communities

Hateful attacks based on protected characteristics, sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, and glorification of abuse are not allowed.

Upload responsibly

Do not upload malware, scams, spam, deceptive impersonation, illegal activity, or copyrighted material without permission. Give viewers accurate titles and descriptions.

Reports and enforcement

Use the Report button when a video breaks these guidelines. Reports are reviewed by authorized moderators, and enforcement may include content removal or account restrictions. Do not abuse reporting tools.
