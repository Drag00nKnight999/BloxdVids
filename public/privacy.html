Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Information we store

We store account information such as your username, password hash, optional email address, role, and account status. Uploaded videos, thumbnails, descriptions, comments, likes, reports, copyright notices and counter-notices, and channel information are stored to provide the service. Copyright submissions may include a legal name, email address, mailing address, telephone number, typed signature, content locations, and legal statements.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate users, operate channels, deliver videos, show engagement counts, moderate the platform, protect the service, and respond to support or legal requests. Copyright submission details are restricted to authorized moderators and administrators for review and response. They may be shared as needed to process a notice or counter-notice or as required by law.

Public information

Usernames, channels, uploaded video metadata, comments, and engagement activity may be visible to other visitors. Your password is never stored in plain text, and your email is not included in the shared account profile response.

Storage and retention

Account and content data is retained while needed to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce platform rules. Deleted content may remain temporarily in backups or logs.

Your choices

You can update your account email in settings and delete videos you own. Contact the platform owner through the available support or report channels for privacy requests.
